# GE19 observational pivot — route toward Nature Astronomy / Nature Physics

**Decision date:** 2026-11-09. **Status:** STRATEGY, NOT OBSERVATIONAL RESULT OR PREDATA LOCK. This document does not alter LW01 PASS, LW02 window-only exploratory result, LW03 radial convergence FAIL, or original H4/Z21 limits.

## Editorial objective

The flagship target is **Nature Astronomy** for a differentiated, independently tested astrophysical signature. **Nature Physics** is appropriate if the central contribution is a genuinely new, compelling physical memory mechanism with an experimentally relevant falsifiable distinction. A flagship **Nature** claim would require wider, unusually decisive implications and replicated observation. Journals do not formally require an observation for all theoretical articles, but the intended project should include a credible one.

Do NOT submit the LW01-only native transfer manuscript as a claimed Nature-level observational discovery. Keep it as a rigorous methods/theory component.

## Research question

> Can a *frozen causal memory kernel* yield a distinctive, testable redshift- and scale-dependent relationship between galaxy clustering, growth/velocity statistics, and gravitational lensing — beyond a generic shift of the lensing amplitude — in public data?

The causal memory law is at bath level: B(t)=integral_retarded f(t-t') dX(t') with positive Drude continuum. LW01 demonstrates nonzero native Weyl transfer tangent but does not establish a distinct observable. One cosmological history may be reproduced by a sufficiently flexible instantaneous time- and scale-dependent effective coupling; therefore do **not** claim the mere detection of nonzero cosmic shear or an apparent modified-gravity fit uniquely proves historical memory. Compare against physically well-defined alternative instantaneous/matched-background models and nuisance physics.

## Three explicit go/no-go gates — no open-ended numerics

**P0: observable theory legitimacy (currently FAIL / incomplete).** Independently resolve radial/source-time accuracy **and** missing full line-of-sight k,z support for a fixed cosmological background, same source convention and gauge as archived original AeST+CLASS. Distinguish externally imposed formal eta tangent from a physically normalized finite coupling if reporting realistic signal size. Use linear-only scale cuts justified independently; account for nonlinear matter, baryons or exclude contaminated scales. Compare baseline against standard public theory/data pipeline without looking for memory improvements. No varying KB, tau H0, p, physical eta or physical CLASS action to accommodate data. No claiming a sign based on higher-N quadrature of the same unresolved source-time operator. If this gate cannot pass within one *pre-committed, bounded* independent resolution campaign, **STOP observational claim**.

**P1: distinctive predictive statistic, frozen BEFORE observed residual inspection.** Derive signed theory templates for at least one survey-space observable from W, P_delta, and growth/velocity, e.g. tomographic galaxy-galaxy tangential shear `gamma_t(theta,z_l,z_s)`, 3x2pt covariance likelihood, or a model-consistent E_G-like ratio. Use a forward model with survey n(z), masks/windows, selection, linear scale cuts and covariances. Explicitly test whether the shape (scale x z dependence, potentially lag-like cross-probe mismatch) is **distinguishable from** changes in S8, galaxy bias, intrinsic alignments, redshift calibration, baryon feedback and instantaneous modified-gravity alternatives. Compute an Asimov / simulation sensitivity or forecast using published covariance before looking at data residuals. Predeclare null controls, negative tests, number of tested features, trials factor and decision rule. If the memory template collapses into S8/bias shifts, do not claim a unique memory signature.

**P2: blind / held-out observations and independent replication.** Reproduce a baseline DES Y3 observable and likelihood before switching memory on; freeze theory, scale cuts, nuisance treatment and decision statistic. Evaluate DES Y3 with residual values masked during development as much as realistically possible; public data mean this is *analysis blinding*, not prospective acquisition. If pilot passes a *strong* falsifiable criterion and is not driven by one tomographic or angular bin, confirm against a genuinely independent DES Y6 data vector when available, with ACT DR6 CMB lensing cross-validation if full theory support permits. Report trials, confidence intervals, likelihood differences, null tests, and disconfirming as well as confirming comparisons. A robust **null constraint** can be scientifically valuable; do not invent a detection.

## Scientific and journal decision

- Native LW01 numerical PASS is a **theoretical prerequisite**, not an observed signal.
- LW03 radial convergence FAIL is a blocking issue for lensing. No test on DES residuals can retroactively pass it.
- DES Y3 is the lowest-friction pilot, because there are public official 3x2pt products and existing user-local gamma_t/observed-covariance preparation. DES Y6 is a better high-statistics confirmation only when the relevant data products are actually accessible and independent.
- The Nature Astronomy/Physics hypothesis must be **the discriminating observable and the model-vs-alternatives evidence**, not a free-amplitude phenomenological fit.
- Go to Nature Astronomy only with a well-validated, convincing independent observational result or especially consequential robust exclusion. Nature Physics if the causal mechanism plus empirical distinguishability is the central discovery. Nature flagship only for qualitatively field-changing and independently robust evidence.
- If P0 or P1 fails, stop the Nature-target observation route and present the bounded conditional analytic bath + native linear transfer study to an appropriate specialist venue after a separate physical derivation/novelty review, not as a lensing detection.
