\[
\theta^0=Ndt,
\qquad

\qquad
\theta^2=R d\theta,

\]

Here `R(t,r)` is the areal radius. Define

\[
e_0=N^{-1}(\partial_t-b\partial_r),

\[

s=\sinh u\,e_0+\cosh u\,e_1.

\[
